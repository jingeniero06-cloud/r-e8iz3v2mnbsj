Executive summary

A strong SEO foundation held back by mobile speed

The site is technically healthy: every sitemap URL resolves, schema coverage is excellent and the blog is deep. The losses are concentrated in three fixable areas.

  1. Mobile speed. The hero image is lazy-loaded and three Google tag libraries block the page. LCP is 14 to 19 s in lab tests.
  2. Index hygiene. Redirected and noindex URLs sit in the sitemap, and demo, duplicate and ad-copy pages are indexable.
  3. Template bugs. The footer phone button on every page links to a broken address, and two county contact buttons lead to a 404.

The five high-priority fixes are configuration and template changes that can ship in about a week.

Top 5 priorities

1

Fix the mobile hero image Performance

WP Rocket lazy-loads the hero background, so the biggest element paints last (LCP 15.1 s on the homepage).

Fix: Exclude the hero container from 'LazyLoad for CSS background images', or use an <img> with fetchpriority=high, and preload it.

Impact High Effort Low
2

Cut third-party JavaScript Performance

GTM, a separate GA4 gtag and a separate Ads gtag load ~530 KB; reCAPTCHA adds 356 KB on every page.

Fix: Fire GA4 and Ads from inside GTM only. Load reCAPTCHA and Formidable only on form pages or on interaction.

Impact High Effort Medium
3

Repair the footer phone button Links

On all 2,120 pages it links to http://(443)%20708-4698, a broken web address.

Fix: Change the href to tel:+14437084698 and remove target=_blank and nofollow.

Impact High Effort Low
4

Clean the XML sitemap Indexing

Lists 14 URLs that redirect elsewhere and 42 job posts marked noindex.

Fix: Remove redirected pages; exclude the careers post type in Yoast or make job pages indexable.

Impact Medium Effort Low
5

Remove duplicate and leftover pages Indexing

A theme demo post, a '-duplicate' page, two '-ga' ad copies and duplicate post pairs are all indexable.

Fix: Delete or 301 the demo and duplicate pages, merge post pairs, noindex the '-ga' pages and /unsubscribe/.

Impact Medium Effort Low

Recommended changes

  • One tag manager. Fire GA4 and Google Ads from inside GTM instead of separate gtag scripts.
  • Forms on demand. Load reCAPTCHA and Formidable only on pages with a form, or on first interaction.
  • Hero first. Exclude the hero from background lazy-load, preload it, serve a 400 px mobile image.
  • Delay marketing pixels until the page is interactive (WP Rocket "Delay JS").

Target after fixes: mobile LCP under 2.5 s and Total Blocking Time under 300 ms.
